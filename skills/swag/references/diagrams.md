# Diagrams

Make the structure or relationship readable before adding detail. Group related items, separate competing paths, and distinguish neighboring groups without relying on color alone. Keep the vocabulary and data exact.

Size containers for their full labels. Give connector lines and direction indicators breathing room, with a clear straight approach to an arrowhead. Keep endpoints clear of bends, labels, and neighboring content.

For an interactive canvas, fit the content to the viewport on load and adapt on resize. Let people pan and zoom across the whole space, centered on their point of interest, while text stays crisp. Preserve the shared highlight, selection, detail, and interaction-guidance principles.

For a static export, make the complete diagram understandable without hover. Put dense supporting detail in a connected reading layer and preserve labels at the actual export size. Prefer editable or vector geometry when it keeps text and connections sharp.

Inspect the full view and dense regions at their delivery size. Check long labels, connection direction, crossings, legends, and complete content. For interactive diagrams, exercise selection, dismissal, dragging, resizing, pan, and zoom; a static capture cannot verify them.
