# Generated images

Define the intended use, subject, composition, visual hierarchy, palette, and output frame before generation. Respect supplied references and preserve required identity, wording, and other invariants during edits. Use the host's image-generation specialist or tool for a raster deliverable.

Let imagery carry the subject rather than substitute generic ornament. Keep the result coherent with the artifact that will use it. Compose for the final crop, display size, and background, including transparency when requested.

Keep factual charts, exact labels, and relationships grounded in their source. Use the chart or diagram route when those are the image's purpose, and prefer precise editable construction for information that generation cannot reproduce reliably. Beauty cannot justify invented data or misspelled required text.

Inspect the generated image itself at its intended size and crop. Check composition, visual artifacts, text accuracy, preserved features, and transparency where relevant. Verify its placement in the consuming artifact. Retain the selected asset in the requested destination and provide a useful description when the medium supports one.
