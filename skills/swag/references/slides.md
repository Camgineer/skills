# Slides

Give each slide a clear purpose in the audience's path through the material. Establish a consistent visual grammar across the deck, with enough variation to show changes in topic or importance. Titles should state the subject or a supported point.

Design for the actual viewing distance and frame. Keep text, labels, charts, and contrast readable in presentation and export. Recompose dense material across slides or into an appendix rather than shrinking it below legibility or cutting it off.

Preserve the fidelity of data, quotes, and sources. Keep charts and diagrams editable where practical, and use their respective swag routes. Add speaker notes only when the request asks for them. The visible slide must still communicate its point when distributed alone.

Use the host's installed presentation companion for authoring and export when available. Inspect every slide at delivery size and in the exported format. Check deck-wide consistency, overflow, reading order, and transitions if they are part of the requested experience. Motion must support comprehension and remain usable in a still export.
